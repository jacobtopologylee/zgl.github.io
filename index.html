Zhiguo Li

Associate Professor, Master's Supervisor

Teaching at the Department of Mathematics, School of Science, Hebei University of Technology. Board member of the Tianjin Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics and zbMATH Open Reviewer. Served as a Visiting Scholar at the National University of Singapore (NUS) from August to November 2015. Awarded the Outstanding Teacher for Undergraduate Teaching Quality at Hebei University of Technology (2019-2025).

My research interests primarily focus on Graph Theory, Applications of Topology and Number Theory. In teaching, I consistently emphasize logical completeness and structural clarity, striving to combine abstract mathematical concepts with geometric intuition and historical motivation.
